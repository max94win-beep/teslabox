FAQ

How do I rent a power bank?
Scan the QR code on the station, pay by card or through the app — the station unlocks the device automatically.

Where can I return the power bank?
At any TeslaBox station, at any time. Insert the device into a free slot until it clicks.

The station won't accept the return — what should I do?
Try another free slot. If none works, message or call us — we will stop the billing manually.

How much does a rental cost?
Current prices are displayed in the app and on the station before you confirm the rental. You are charged only for the time of use.

What if I don't return the device?
After the maximum rental period expires, the full device price is charged and the device is yours. Details are in the Terms of Use.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Delete account, or by request to our e-mail. Deleting the account also deletes your data in accordance with the Privacy Policy.
