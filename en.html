Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the app?

Not necessarily — you can rent a power bank by scanning the QR code and paying by card. The app makes it easier to find stations and track your rental.

Which phones does it charge?

All of them. Every TeslaBox power bank has three built-in cables: USB-C, Lightning and Micro-USB.

What if I don’t return the power bank?

After the maximum rental period expires, the full device price is charged and the power bank is yours. You see the amount before renting.

Can I return the power bank at a different station?

Yes — that’s the point. Pick up at one station, return at any other TeslaBox station.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay — cashless only.
