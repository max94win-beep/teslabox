Data we collect

  • Account data: phone number and/or e-mail address provided at registration.
  • Usage data: rental history — pickup and return station, rental duration, amount charged.
  • Location: approximate device location, used solely to show the nearest stations; processed only with your permission granted in your phone settings.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, IP address — for correct operation and security of the service.

Payment card data

We do not collect or store payment card data (card number, expiry date, CVV). Card data is entered and processed exclusively on the certified payment page of the payment service provider (payment gateway) operating under the PCI DSS standard. We receive only the transaction status confirmation and a billing token from which card data cannot be reconstructed.

Purpose and legal basis of processing

  • Performance of the rental contract (registration, billing, support) — Art. 12(1)(2) ZZPL;
  • Compliance with legal obligations (accounting, fiscalization) — Art. 12(1)(3) ZZPL;
  • Legitimate interest (abuse prevention, system security) — Art. 12(1)(6) ZZPL.

Who processes data on our behalf

Data is processed on our behalf by: the technical platform of the service (server and application), the payment service provider and hosting providers. Data processing agreements are in place with all processors. We do not sell data to third parties and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Retention

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. Transaction data is kept for the periods required by the accounting and tax regulations of the Republic of Serbia.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can delete your account and all related data in the app (option "Delete account") or by request to sales@teslabox.rs. We respond to requests within the statutory period.

If you believe processing is unlawful, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade, poverenik.rs).

Changes

We will notify you of material changes to this Policy via the app or e-mail. The current version is always published on this page.
